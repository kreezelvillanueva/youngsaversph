## WHAT THIS PRODUCT IS ABOUT

Improved productivity and boosted company morale are products of happy and healthy employees. This is why providing medical benefits for your workforce is key to their productivity.

Health Link is a comprehensive plan that offers medical and life insurance benefits to business owners with at least 5 regular employees.

- ### BASIC MEDICAL / INPATIENT BENEFIT

  Covers the employee’s expenses during confinement and pays for the following services:
  - Room & Board daily charges including ICU

  - Other hospital services including lab tests, X-ray tests, physical therapy, oxygen, anesthesia, central supplies and medicines during confinement

  - Surgical Fees

  - Doctor's Calls
