Be all out in protecting yourself and your family.

With AIA All-In-One, you get protected against 4 major risks in life: untimely death, accidents, disability and critical illnesses. That way, you can conveniently secure your future and live worry-free with your family. Your benefits are not affected by market risks, so you can get full protection when the need arises.

The plan gives a cash payout to your loved ones in case of an untimely death, and cash support in case of accidents, disabilities and critical illnesses.\*

\*Payment of benefits is subject to the terms, conditions, definitions, exclusions and limitations found in the policy contract.
