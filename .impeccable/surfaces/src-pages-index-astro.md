# Homepage surface brief

Scope: `/` (homepage). Mode: Persuade. Audience: young Filipino earners and families arriving on phones from Facebook/Messenger. Action: book the free online assessment (Calendly) or message on Messenger.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Insurance, made simple: a polished, modern advisor site (the category standard, executed at Wise/Maya craft) where the advisor is a guide, not the hero. Refuses the default "giant advisor portrait + three icon cards" arrangement.
OWN-WORLD: Deep maroon #8b1538 primary with maroon-ink #5c0d25, blush #f8eef0 tints, white and warm light gray grounds, near-black ink. Rounded 20–32px cards, soft maroon-tinted shadows, pill buttons and pill labels, white-bordered photos overlapping a maroon block. Bold grotesk display (Onest-family), friendly UI sans body.
STORY: The visitor sees "Insurance, made simple.", understands the three goals (Savings, Protection, Investment) from real photos, sees that it's fully online and assessment-first, trusts the named guide (small "Your guide" pill), and books or messages.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: "Your guide" pill (small circular avatar, name, license line), headline "Insurance, made simple." with "simple." in maroon, one-paragraph lede, primary maroon "Book a free online assessment" button and secondary Messenger button, two assurance chips. Right: maroon rounded block with three overlapping old-site photos (Protection large top-left, Savings right, Investment bottom-left), each with a white pill label, settling in on load in sequence.
FORM: Category standard (canon), user-steered from a safer re-roll: layout from the "Product-style cards" mock with the hero visual from the "Photo-led" mock. Seed key 27998ae1. Approved reference: .impeccable/mocks/directions/std-final.html (desktop/mobile captures alongside).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
